How credits work

Log a chore in up to three taps and earn credits. Bigger jobs pay more and need a photo that an admin reviews.

  • Cutlery back in the drawer+1
  • Dishwasher+2
  • Coffee grounds to the basement+3
  • Bring milk+3
  • Bring coffee beans+5
  • Descale the coffee machine+10
  • Deep clean the kitchen or fridge+15

Each chore has a short cooldown, so credits stay fair for everyone.
